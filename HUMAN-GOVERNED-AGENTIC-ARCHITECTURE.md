# ZIATec Human-Governed Agentic Architecture

**Doctrine version: 2026-09-12**

ZIATec's enterprise architecture philosophy places accountable human authority above automated capability.

**AI supplies velocity. People retain authority.**

## Enterprise control plane
**Client → Contract → Scope → Policy → Identity → Agent → Evidence → Human Authority → Client**

AI agents operate under least privilege, explicit scope, evidence capture and graduated authorization.

### Authority tiers
0. Observe.
1. Recommend.
2. Generate implementation artifacts without execution.
3. Execute after specific human approval.
4. Execute only pre-authorized, reversible, low-blast-radius actions with logging and rollback.

## Architecture requirements
Identity, policy enforcement, observability, recovery verification, provenance, human approval boundaries, client isolation, audit evidence and rollback are structural requirements. RAG and model output are context, not authority.

## Executive advisory boundary
ZIATec provides architecture, security posture analysis, governance, roadmaps and other professional services under explicit engagement scope. Operational authority remains where the governing agreement places it. Automated output does not represent independent compliance certification or regulatory determination.

## Commercial model
Professional services use defensible units such as advisory hours, architecture sprints, assessments, managed services and fixed deliverables. Infrastructure/model consumption is separately metered or transparently included rather than represented as human labor.

## Position
**Enterprise architecture without enterprise overhead.** Human-accountable AI agents perform bounded research, engineering, security, compliance and operational analysis under a unified evidence and authorization plane.
