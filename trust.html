ZIATEC · ENTERPRISE TRUST ARCHITECTURE

Human authority above automated capability.

AI supplies velocity. People retain authority.

Client → Contract → Scope → Policy → Identity → Agent → Evidence → Human Authority → Client

Graduated authority

Tier 0 Observe · Tier 1 Recommend · Tier 2 Generate without execution · Tier 3 Human-approved execution · Tier 4 narrowly pre-authorized reversible autonomy with complete evidence and rollback.

Architecture requirements

Identity, policy enforcement, observability, recovery verification, provenance, client isolation, human approval boundaries, audit evidence and rollback are structural requirements. RAG and model output are context, not authority.

Advisory boundary

Architecture, security posture analysis, governance and roadmaps operate under explicit engagement scope. Automated output does not constitute independent compliance certification or regulatory determination.
